# Your context, yours

openmemfs is the **context of your personal agent**: what it knows about you, your work and how you
like things done, kept as files you can read and edit.

- **It is yours.** It runs on your server, in your database. You decide what goes in, how it is
  organised and who reaches it.
- **It is the piece that stays.** Agents and harnesses come and go: Claude Code today, another one
  tomorrow. Your context should not move with them. Connect any harness to openmemfs and it starts
  from what you already wrote.
- **It is personal software.** One person and their agents, not a product for many users. That is
  why it has no accounts and no login out of the box: you add what you need.
- **It is code, and you own it.** Fork it, change it and grow it with modules. With an agent at your
  side, extending it is a conversation: ask for a tab, a tool or a page and review the change.

You are responsible for keeping it, and free to make it whatever you need. Keep one thing true:
whatever you add, openmemfs stays your context, plain files that you and your agents read and
write.
